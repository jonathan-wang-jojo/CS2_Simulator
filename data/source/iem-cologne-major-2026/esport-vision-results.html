About the tournament

IEM Cologne Major 2026 is a Counter-Strike 2 tournament, 2 Jun – 21 Jun (LAN · Germany). 34 teams take part. The prize pool is $1,170,000.

Stages: Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, Playoffs. This page has the schedule and results of every stage, the group standings and the playoff bracket.

The tournament was won by Team Falcons.
